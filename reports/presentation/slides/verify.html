Before trusting real data, I checked the code against cases where I know the answer. Against the analytical solution, Euler converges at order 0.97 and RK4 at 4.14, matching theory. Euler goes oscillatory above one time constant and diverges above two, as the amplification factor 1 minus dt over tau predicts, so production uses the exact solution, which is unconditionally stable. On manufactured rooms the calibration recovers V and Q within about 5%. The synthetic study also exposed a timing bug: labelling interval-mean occupancy at the interval start biased V by about 40%; centring it fixed that. All of this runs as 27 pytest tests.
