The audit changed the project. Three of five env sensors were frozen, re-sending one payload tens of thousands of times: 76% of rows were duplicates. The location sheet didn't link any working sensor to a counter, and its occupancy tab is labelled invalid. So I paired them from the data: correlation of CO2 and headcount after subtracting each series' time-of-week mean, so that 'both busy in office hours' can't create a match. One pair stood out at r = 0.65 over about 25 labelled days; two weaker pairs became controls. The 'Do not use' example data was not used.
