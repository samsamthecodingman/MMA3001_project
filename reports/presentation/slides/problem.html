Can the CO₂ sensors we already have count people?

Input

CO₂ [ppm], temperature, humidity, irregular ~10 min

Estimator

Physics mass balance or ML regressor, calibrated on counter data

Output

Occupants per 15 min, plus an occupied/vacant flag

HVAC ventilates to a timetable that assumes full rooms. People-counters are expensive and rare; CO₂ sensors are cheap and common. A trustworthy “virtual counter” enables demand-controlled ventilation.

Ventilation is one of the biggest energy loads in a university building, and it usually runs to a timetable. Demand-controlled ventilation needs to know how many people are actually in each zone. Counters are rare; CO2 sensors are everywhere. So: input is CO2, temperature and humidity every ~10 minutes; output is an occupancy estimate every 15 minutes. Intended use: ventilation scheduling and utilisation reports, not safety-critical counting.
