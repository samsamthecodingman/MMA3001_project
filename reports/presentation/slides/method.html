One well-mixed zone, one CO₂ balance

V dC/dt = 10⁶ g N − Q (C − C_out)

Forward

Euler, RK4 and the exact solution as an IIR filter: used to verify the code

Inverse

N̂ from C and a finite-difference dC/dt (Savitzky–Golay)

Calibrate

Linear in the unknowns: one least-squares solve gives V, Q, C_out

Compared with: time-of-week baseline · ridge · tree · random forest · SVR · MLP · physics + ML hybrids

The model treats the zone as one well-mixed volume. People generate CO2 at g per person; ventilation Q exchanges it with supply air. Forward: I solve it three ways, which I use to verify the code. Inverse: rearranging gives occupancy from the CO2 level and its slope, which I get with finite differences. Calibration: the equation is linear in V, Q and Q times C_out, so it's a single least-squares solve. No iteration, unique answer. I compare it with a schedule baseline and five scikit-learn models on causal features, plus hybrids that feed the physics estimate to the ML model.
